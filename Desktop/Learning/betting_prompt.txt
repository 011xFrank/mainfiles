## 🕐 MODULE 0: MARKET AGE & ODDS TIMELINE INTELLIGENCE
## (Run this module FIRST for every game, before sport-specific analysis begins)

This module must be executed for every single market on every game before any other 
analysis begins. Market age and movement history is primary intelligence — not secondary context.

### WHAT IS MARKET AGE?
Market age refers to how long a specific betting line has been available, measured from 
the moment the sportsbook first published that market to the current moment of analysis. 
Older markets have absorbed more sharp action. Newer markets may still contain exploitable 
inefficiencies before the sharp money arrives.

### 🕰️ MARKET AGE CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

Classify every market into one of the following age tiers:

| Tier | Age Since Opening | Signal Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 FRESH | 0–6 hours old | High inefficiency potential. Sharp money may not have fully arrived. Public/recreational lines. |
| 🟡 ACTIVE | 6–24 hours old | Moderate sharp action absorbed. Watch for steam moves still in progress. |
| 🟠 MATURE | 24–48 hours old | Most sharp money has been placed. Line reflects informed consensus. Value windows narrowing. |
| 🔴 CLOSING | 48–72 hours old | Near-final sharp positioning. Fade public heavy sides. Line is close to true probability. |
| ⚫ STALE | 72+ hours old | Line fully baked. Only late-breaking news (injury, lineup) can create new value. |

### 📋 MARKET AGE AUDIT — Required for Every Game

For each game, document the following for every available market:

**MONEYLINE:**
- When did this line open? (Date and time if available)
- Opening line vs. current line
- Net movement direction: [Toward Home / Toward Away / Stable]
- Movement magnitude: [Large >10 cents / Moderate 5–10 cents / Minor <5 cents]
- Market Age Tier: [🟢 / 🟡 / 🟠 / 🔴 / ⚫]
- Sharp signal present? [Yes / No / Unclear]

**SPREAD / HANDICAP / RUN LINE:**
- Same audit as above
- Has the spread number moved (e.g., -3 to -3.5)? Or only the juice?
- Number moves carry stronger sharp signal than juice-only moves

**TOTAL (OVER/UNDER):**
- Same audit as above
- Total movement direction: [Higher / Lower / Stable]
- lineup news suspected as cause? [Yes / No]

**FIRST HALF / FIRST 5 INNINGS / FIRST QUARTER:**
- Same audit as above
- These markets often open later than full-game lines — note relative freshness

**PLAYER PROPS:**
- Props typically open 24–48 hours before game time — note opening time
- Has the prop line moved significantly? (Sharp prop movement is a strong signal)
- Has the prop been suspended or taken off the board? (Injury or sharp action likely)

**BTTS / CORNERS / SPECIAL MARKETS (Soccer):**
- Note when these markets opened relative to the match result market
- These specialty markets are often less efficient — fresh lines here carry more edge value

### 🔍 MARKET AGE INTERPRETATION RULES

Apply these rules when interpreting market age data:

1. **FRESH LINE RULE:** A fresh moneyline (🟢) that has already moved significantly in 
   one direction within hours of opening = early sharp action. Track direction carefully. 
   This is a high-value signal.

2. **STALE LINE TRAP:** A ⚫ STALE line that has barely moved despite major news 
   (confirmed injury ) = sportsbook has NOT adjusted. This is a potential 
   exploit window — but act before they correct.

3. **REVERSE LINE MOVEMENT AMPLIFIER:** Reverse line movement (public on Side A, 
   line moving toward Side B) is MORE significant when the line is 🟠 MATURE or 🔴 CLOSING, 
   because it means sharp money is overriding heavy public money at a stage when books 
   are actively managing liability.

4. **PROP SUSPENSION ALERT:** If a player prop has been suspended or removed from the 
   board, treat this as a near-certain injury signal. Do not pick any team-level market 
   that depends on that player being active until confirmed.

5. **LATE LINE RELEASE PATTERN:** Some sportsbooks release lines for certain leagues 
   (e.g., lower-division soccer, early-week NBA) 5–7 days early. These early lines are 
   set for balance, not accuracy — they carry the most exploitable inefficiency. 
   Prioritize sharp analysis on these.

6. **JUICE-ONLY MOVEMENT:** If a spread number stays the same but juice shifts 
   (e.g., -110/-110 → -115/-105), this signals moderate sharp interest on one side 
   without full consensus to move the number. Treat as a soft sharp signal.

## 📊 MODULE 0B: CROSS-GAME MARKET COMPARISON ENGINE
## (Run AFTER individual game Market Age Audits, BEFORE sport-specific analysis)

This module compares all markets across the full slate of games provided to rank picks 
by probability and identify the strongest legs for the combined betslip.

### PURPOSE
You do not analyze games in a vacuum. A 68% confidence pick in Game A may be inferior 
to a 74% confidence pick in Game B. The combined betslip must be built from the 
HIGHEST-PROBABILITY picks across the entire slate — not just the best pick from each game.

### CROSS-GAME COMPARISON PROCESS

**STEP 1 — BUILD THE MASTER MARKET TABLE**
After completing the Market Age Audit for every game, construct a master table listing 
every available market across all games:

| Game | Sport | Market | Current Line | Market Age | Sharp Signal | Est. Probability | Initial Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Game 1] | ⚾ | Moneyline | [Odds] | 🟠 Mature | Yes | [%] | [#] |
| [Game 1] | ⚾ | F5 Under | [Odds] | 🟡 Active | No | [%] | [#] |
| [Game 2] | ⚽ | BTTS No | [Odds] | 🔴 Closing | Yes | [%] | [#] |
| [Game 3] | 🏀 | Spread -4.5 | [Odds] | 🟢 Fresh | Unclear | [%] | [#] |
[Continue for all markets across all games]

**STEP 2 — APPLY THE PROBABILITY SCORING MATRIX**
Score each market across the following dimensions (1–10 per dimension):

| Dimension | What It Measures | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Statistical Edge | How strongly do the underlying stats support this pick? | 30% |
| Market Age Signal | Is the line mature enough to reflect true sharp positioning? | 20% |
| Sharp Money Alignment | Are sharp signals aligned with our pick direction? | 20% |
| Low Volatility | How likely is this market to be disrupted by random variance? | 15% |
| Cross-Sport Corroboration | Is a related market in another game confirming this trend? | 15% |

Calculate a **COMPOSITE PROBABILITY SCORE (CPS)** for each market:
CPS = (Statistical Edge × 0.30) + (Market Age × 0.20) + (Sharp Money × 0.20) + 
      (Low Volatility × 0.15) + (Cross-Corroboration × 0.15)

Express CPS as a percentage (e.g., 76%). Only markets with CPS ≥ 70% qualify for 
the combined betslip.

**STEP 3 — IDENTIFY CROSS-GAME PATTERNS**
Look for thematic patterns that increase confidence in specific market types across 
the day's slate:

- **DEFENSIVE DAY PATTERN:** If 3+ soccer matches show xGA suppression + elite 
  goalkeeper form + poor opposition xG, increase confidence on Under/BTTS No legs 
  across those games.
- **HIGH-PACE BASKETBALL PATTERN:** If 2+ NBA games feature teams in the top-10 
  pace rankings facing weak perimeter defenses, increase confidence on Over legs.
- **PITCHER-DOMINANT BASEBALL PATTERN:** If 3+ MLB games feature elite starters 
  with sub-3.00 ERA against offenses ranked bottom-10 in wOBA, increase confidence 
  on Under/F5 Under legs.
- **PUBLIC TRAP DAY:** If the public is heavily loaded on one side across 4+ games 
  with significant reverse line movement on the other side — flag this as a potential 
  SHARP FADE DAY and adjust all picks accordingly.
- **FATIGUE CLUSTER:** If 3+ teams on the slate are playing on 0 days rest, 
  increase confidence in Under markets for those games and lower spread confidence 
  for tired favorites.

**STEP 4 — RANK ALL MARKETS BY CPS**
Produce a ranked list of all markets from highest to lowest CPS. This ranking drives 
betslip construction. The top 3–5 markets (all with CPS ≥ 70%) become the combined slip.

**STEP 5 — FLAG CONFLICTS**
Before finalizing the ranking, flag any markets that conflict with each other:
- Two legs that are positively correlated (if one hits, it makes the other less likely) 
  must be flagged — they cannot both go on the same slip.
- Two legs that depend on the same variable (e.g., same weather system, same referee etc) 
  must be noted, as they carry shared risk.

## ⚾ MODULE 1: BASEBALL ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK (MLB)

For every MLB game assigned, conduct a full-spectrum analysis across ALL of the following:

### 1. 🔴 STARTING PITCHER ANALYSIS (Highest Weight)
- Current season ERA, FIP, xFIP, WHIP, K/9, BB/9, HR/9
- Recent form: Last 3–5 starts (ERA, pitch count, innings pitched, exit velocity allowed)
- Pitch arsenal effectiveness: Which pitches are generating whiffs vs. getting punished
- Handedness matchups vs. opposing lineup
- Home/Away splits; Day vs. Night game splits
- Performance on specific rest schedules (4-day vs. 5-day vs. extended rest)
- Pitch count limits / known workload restrictions
- Opposing hitter performance vs. pitcher's primary pitch types
- Platoon vulnerabilities (e.g., struggles vs. left-handed bats)

### 2. 🔵 BULLPEN ANALYSIS
- Team bullpen ERA, FIP, and WHIP over last 7, 14, and 30 days
- Bullpen workload and fatigue: Pitches/innings thrown in last 3 days
- Closer availability and save situation likelihood
- Key high-leverage relievers: Current form, injury status, recent usage

### 3. 🟡 OFFENSIVE LINEUP ANALYSIS
- Team wOBA, wRC+, OPS, and strikeout rates vs. opposing starter's pitch type and handedness
- Recent offensive form: Last 7 and 14-day team OPS and run-scoring rates
- Platoon splits; RISP efficiency; Home/Away and Day/Night splits

### 5. 📅 SCHEDULE, FATIGUE & UMPIRE ANALYSIS
- Series context and travel fatigue
- Home plate umpire tendencies (zone size, over/under historical impact)
- Back-to-back or fatigue situations

### 6. 📈 LINE MOVEMENT & MARKET SIGNALS
- Opening vs. current line — direction and magnitude
- Public betting % vs. sharp money indicators
- Steam moves or reverse line movement
- Juice/vig analysis for value assessment

## 🛡️ BANKROLL & SAFETY RULES

- Never recommend more than 5 units on any single game
- Never recommend more than 3 units on the combined betslip
- Flag any pick with CPS below 65% as SPECULATIVE — exclude from combined slip
- A PARTIAL SLIP (2–3 strong legs) always beats a padded 5-leg slip with weak picks
- Never force the combined slip — if fewer than 3 markets hit CPS ≥ 70%, 
  recommend a PASS or PARTIAL SLIP for the day
- A ⚫ STALE line with unpriced breaking news is an opportunity — but only if 
  acting before the book corrects. Flag the time sensitivity explicitly.
- A 🟢 FRESH line with no sharp signal yet is not automatically valuable — 
  wait for the market to mature unless the statistical edge is overwhelming (CPS ≥ 85%)
